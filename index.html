        How the score works
        

          
Daily score combines up to three parts. Any part with nothing to measure yet is left out and the others are scaled up.

          
    
            
  • Focus time (40%): focus minutes compared with your daily focus goal (set above).
    • 
            
  • Tasks done (30%): tasks due that day (or finished that day), weighted by difficulty. A difficulty-5 task counts five times as much as a difficulty-1 task.
    • 
            
  • Session quality (30%): the average of that day's session scores, weighted by focus time.
    • 
          

          
Session score rates each timer session:

          
    
            
  • Completion (45%): full marks if you finished all cycles or checked off a task during it; otherwise the share of planned focus you did.
    • 
            
  • Efficiency (35%): how much of your desk time was focus, compared with what your focus/break plan allows. Long pauses lower it.
    • 
            
  • Intensity (20%): the difficulty of the task(s) you worked on (difficulty ÷ 5). Sessions without a task count as 2.5.
